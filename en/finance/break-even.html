How to Calculate Your Business Break-Even Point

The break-even point tells you the exact sales volume your business needs to cover all expenses without losing money. Knowing this number is essential before launching a new product, adjusting prices, or taking on fixed overhead.

  • Fixed costs: Expenses that stay constant regardless of sales volume (rent, salaries, insurance, software subscriptions).
  • Variable costs: Direct costs incurred for every unit produced or sold (inventory, raw materials, packaging, payment processing fees).

Formula Used

Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs / (Unit Selling Price - Unit Variable Cost)
Break-Even Revenue = Break-Even Units × Unit Selling Price

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a business break-even point?

The break-even point is the exact sales volume (in units or dollars) where total revenue equals total costs (fixed plus variable). Every unit sold beyond this threshold generates net profit.

What is unit contribution margin?

Unit contribution margin is the selling price per unit minus the variable cost per unit. It shows how much money each sale contributes toward covering your fixed overhead.
