How does compound interest work?

Unlike simple interest—which only calculates returns on your original principal—compound interest reinvests your earnings each period so that your interest generates additional interest over time.

Formula used

FV = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t) + PMT × [ ((1 + r/n)^(n×t) - 1) / (r/n) ]

Where P is your initial deposit, PMT is the monthly contribution, r is the annual interest rate in decimal form, n is compounding periods per year (12), and t is time in years.

Frequently Asked Questions

What matters more: interest rate or time?

Over longer horizons (10+ years), time generally outweighs small rate differences because exponential compounding accelerates most sharply in later years.
