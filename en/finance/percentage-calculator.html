Quick Guide to Percentages and Discounts

Whether you are shopping during a sale, pricing merchandise, or tracking sales growth, three core percentage formulas handle everyday math accurately:

  • Applying a discount: Multiply the original price by (1 - Discount % / 100). For example, a 25% discount means multiplying the price by 0.75.
  • Finding a percentage of a value: Multiply the base amount by the percentage and divide by 100.
  • Percentage change: Measure growth or decline using [(Final - Initial) / Initial] × 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate final price with discount and sales tax?

First subtract the discount percentage from the original price to get the discounted subtotal, then apply the sales tax percentage to that subtotal.

How do you calculate percentage change between two values?

Subtract the initial value from the final value, divide the difference by the initial value, and multiply by 100. A positive number shows an increase while a negative number indicates a decrease.
