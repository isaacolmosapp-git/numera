Profit Margin vs. Markup Explained

A common pricing mistake is confusing profit margin with markup:

  • Profit margin: The percentage of the net selling price that remains as profit.
  • Markup: The percentage added on top of your unit cost to arrive at the selling price.

Formula Used

Net Price = Cost / (1 - Margin %)
Final Price = Net Price × (1 + Tax %)

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is sales tax added at the end?

Sales tax or VAT is collected on behalf of tax authorities and is not part of your revenue or margin. Your net price must be set first to protect your profit.

What is a healthy profit margin?

It varies by industry: retail typically ranges from 25% to 50%, while digital products and professional services often achieve 70% or higher due to low variable costs.
