How to Evaluate Investment ROI and CAGR

Measuring the true performance of an investment, marketing campaign, or business venture requires looking at both total cumulative gains and how long it took to achieve them:

  • ROI (Return on Investment): Shows your total percentage return relative to all capital invested (including fees, maintenance, or closing costs).
  • CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate): Converts your total return into a steady annual growth rate, letting you fairly compare an 8-month deal against a 4-year investment.

Formulas Used

ROI (%) = [(Final Value - Total Invested) / Total Invested] × 100
CAGR (%) = [(Final Value / Total Invested)^(1 / Years) - 1] × 100

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Return on Investment (ROI)?

ROI measures the total percentage gain or loss relative to your total capital invested. It is calculated by dividing net profit by total investment cost and multiplying by 100.

What is the difference between ROI and CAGR?

ROI reflects total cumulative return regardless of timeframe, whereas CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) measures the smoothed annualized return, allowing you to compare investments of different durations.
