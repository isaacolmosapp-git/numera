How to Plan a Realistic Savings Goal

Breaking a large financial milestone into fixed monthly or weekly transfers removes guesswork and lets you automate savings right on payday.

Savings Deposit Formula

Monthly Deposit = (Goal - Starting Balance) / Months

If your funds sit in a high-yield savings account, compound interest contributes toward your target and lowers the amount you need to deposit out of pocket.

Frequently Asked Questions

How large should an emergency fund be?

Most financial planners recommend saving 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses in an easily accessible account.
